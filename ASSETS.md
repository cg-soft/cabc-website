# Assets and usage notes

## Included files

- `client/public/images/club-logo.png`: derived from the logo supplied for the
  club project. The red background was adjusted to match the approved header
  button color while retaining the artwork.
- `client/public/favicon.png`: small version of the club logo.
- `client/public/images/bridge-table.webp`: approved composite hero image,
  combining an AI-generated bridge-table scene with a red plastic duplicate-board
  product photograph.

The board product reference is a Baron Barclay Imperial duplicate board
([product listing](https://www.amazon.com/Baron-Barclay-Imperial-Duplicate-Boards/dp/B01JB0FLSK)).
The composited red-board photo was retrieved from this
[product image URL](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/61XRlFFOz6L._AC_UF894,1000_QL80_.jpg).

The original reference search also used the
[Baron Barclay Imperial Plus listing](https://baronbarclay.com/products/imperial-plus-duplicate-boards).

## Rights review before public launch

Including an asset in this source handoff does not establish a license for its
public commercial use. Confirm that the club has permission to use the supplied
logo and the third-party product photograph, or replace the board imagery with
an authorized photograph before public launch. This handoff does not assert an
endorsement by the manufacturer or that the hero is a photograph of a real club
game.

External fonts are referenced in `client/index.html`; their files are not
bundled. Review the relevant font licenses and privacy requirements if you
intend to self-host them.

The package retains the template's `license: MIT` metadata. That metadata does
not relicense third-party dependencies, fonts, the club logo or product imagery.
Review their respective notices and terms before redistribution.
